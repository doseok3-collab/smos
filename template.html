Soft-matter Optical Spectroscopy Laboratory

Molecules at interfaces, read by light

We use nonlinear and vibrational spectroscopy, chiefly sum-frequency generation, to see how molecules, ions and water organize at soft-matter interfaces: lipid and surfactant monolayers, charged surfaces and ionic liquids.

Prof. Doseok Kim · Department of Physics, Sogang University, Seoul

Research

An interface is a few molecular layers thick. Sum-frequency generation is forbidden in centrosymmetric bulk media, so its signal comes from exactly those layers.

{{RESEARCH}}

Earlier work in the lab included time-resolved fluorescence, optical Kerr gate dynamics, single-molecule FRET and FCS of RecA filaments, and two-photon interference.
