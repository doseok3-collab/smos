### Charged interfaces and the electric double layer

*SFG · Langmuir monolayers · air/water*

How ions arrange themselves under a charged monolayer. We follow the interfacial water (OH stretch, 3000–3600 cm⁻¹) and the headgroups of fatty acids, amines and cationic lipids as salt, pH and surface density change: protonation promoted by salt, charge condensation, and charge inversion by multivalent and even hydroxide ions.

### Ionic liquids at surfaces and buried interfaces

*SFG · X-ray reflectivity · IR*

Surface ordering and layering of room-temperature ionic liquids at the air, at buried liquid/liquid interfaces and on Pt electrodes, together with the bulk structure of their mixtures with water and the ion pairing that persists in non-polar solvents.

### Isolated molecules and water dimers

*IR · 2D IR · matrix isolation in CCl₄*

Water and small molecules diluted in an inert solvent, studied by infrared and 2D IR spectroscopy at room temperature: the water dimer, coupling of symmetric and antisymmetric stretches, and molecules carrying dimethyl groups.

### Phase-resolved nonlinear spectroscopy

*Instrumentation · ultrafast optics*

We build our own light sources and spectrometers: optical parametric amplification of infrared pulses and heterodyne-detected SFG, which measures the complex χ⁽²⁾ spectrum and with it the up/down orientation of interfacial molecules.
